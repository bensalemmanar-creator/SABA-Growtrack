## 💡 The idea

**The problem.** Small and medium farmers in Tunisia struggle to obtain bank loans. Banks cannot easily verify the state of a farm or the risk of a crop, and farmers lack documented, trusted data to support their loan applications.

**The solution.** SABA GrowTrack connects three actors on one platform:

| Actor | What they get |
|---|---|
| 🧑‍🌾 **Farmers** | Crop-stage guidance, reminders, faster financing, access to a marketplace |
| 🔬 **Agronomists** | A portal to follow farms, integrate drone scan data, and prepare verified loan files |
| 🏦 **Banks** | A portal with verified farmer portfolios, live crop health and risk analytics |

Drone scans (RoboCare) and agronomist inputs are turned into crop-health data, which is used to build verified loan files and a risk view for the bank.

---

## ✨ Main features

- **Smart Crop Monitoring**: drone + agronomist inputs → crop health zones, alerts, growth stages, downloadable reports
- **Financing Automation**: auto-generated loan files, integrated risk score, yield prediction, bank approval workflow
- **PlantRight Guidance**: crop-stage instructions, reminders, agronomist notes, action suggestions based on scan results
- **Smart Marketplace**: inputs marketplace and pre-harvest sales
- **Multi-dashboard system**: tailored portals for agronomists and banks

### Screens designed in Figma

- **Agronomist portal:** dashboard, field file with growth timeline, drone insights, marketplace, pre-harvest marketing, loan files, messages & alerts
- **Bank portal:** portfolio overview, farmer profile, risk dashboard, reports

## 🧭 Design Thinking process

The project follows the design thinking approach taught in the course:

1. Defining and refining the problem scope (farmers' access to credit)
2. Root-cause analysis
3. Personas and customer journey
4. Expert interview (director of a local agricultural company)
5. Value propositions
6. Product vision, backlog, roadmap and marketing campaign
7. Prototype in Figma

---

## 🗺️ Roadmap

1. **Foundation**: partnerships (SABA + RoboCare), farmer research, data model definition
2. **Monitoring prototype**: drone-to-dashboard pipeline, agronomist portal MVP, basic alerts
3. **Financial integration**: automatic loan files, bank dashboards, first credit scoring model
4. **Market expansion**: inputs marketplace, pre-harvest sales, multi-dashboards finalized, launch
